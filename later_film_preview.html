The Story

What Is Later About?

"A Year 12 student watches his dream science scholarship go to a classmate after months of telling himself he'll apply tomorrow — until a second chance forces him to choose between his excuses and his future."

The film follows Rhys, a 17-year-old student who is smart, aware of his own potential, but stuck in a pattern of delaying things he cares about. He has his eye on a prestigious science scholarship — but every time he sits down to apply, he tells himself: "I'm not ready yet." "I'll do it later." "I'll start when I have more experience."

Days pass. Games. Short videos. Distractions. While he waits to feel ready, his classmate Maya quietly gets to work — and wins the scholarship Rhys never applied for.

When a second scholarship appears, Rhys faces the same choice again. This time, the film shows him doing something different: he puts the phone down, opens his laptop, and starts.

Later is about a habit almost every young person has — delaying things they care about because they are waiting to feel "ready." The film shows the real cost of that habit: not just lost time, but lost opportunities. But it does not end on a dark note.

"The best time to start was yesterday.
The second best time is right now."
